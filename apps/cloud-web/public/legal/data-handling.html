Technical data handling

This describes the hosted application's implemented behavior. It is not a complete hosted-service contract or privacy policy. Legal seller information, private support routing, refund terms and infrastructure backup retention must be configured before a public paid launch.

Workspace information

The hosted app stores your company profile, contacts, investor records, drafts, tasks, meetings, source notes, approved actions, and saved documents on its server. Workspace members, including viewers, can read and export shared CRM information. Selecting a document uploads it; it does not remain only on your computer.

Connected services

Eliza Cloud handles login, app subscriptions, inference and delegated Google access. Delegated session credentials are encrypted in the app database. Each member selects their own Google mailbox; imported relationship activity becomes part of the shared workspace. AI receives the CRM context you select for a request. Provider processing is also subject to the applicable provider's policies.

Retention controls in this app

Unlinked uploads expire after 24 hours. Generated downloads and archive transfers expire after one hour. Saved documents remain until removed or workspace content is deleted. The server runs periodic cleanup; expiration also prevents further downloads. Request-rate buckets are removed after ten minutes. Product activity records use action names and workspace identifiers, not a second copy of message bodies or document contents.

Permanent workspace content removal deletes active CRM snapshots, saved files, mailbox selections, invitations and retained AI response content. Minimal membership, billing and audit records remain. It does not recall messages, delete provider calendars, remove shared Eliza identity, erase other apps, or itself remove older infrastructure backups. No infrastructure backup-retention period is asserted here.

Your controls

You can export records, download an encrypted complete cloud archive, remove documents, disconnect a mailbox, disable background sync, sign out all Outreachr sessions, leave a workspace, or have an owner archive or delete its content. Archiving and deletion are separate from subscription cancellation.
