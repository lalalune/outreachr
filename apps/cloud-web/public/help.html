Using the hosted workspace

Outreachr helps founders organize investor research, review outreach, and follow up with their team. The hosted app stores shared workspace data on its server. Eliza Cloud supplies account access, app billing, inference and Google connections.

Get to your first reviewed draft

  1. Sign in with Eliza and complete your company and round profile.
  2. Review an investor's dated sources, current role and contact address. Add them to your shortlist.
  3. Save company context and select what AI may use. AI proposes changes from that context; it does not browse or verify current facts.
  4. In workspace settings, select your own Gmail account and enter your real sender postal address.
  5. Review the exact recipient, subject, body and footer. Approve, then send. Changing the content invalidates approval.
  6. Sync mail to import relevant relationship events, or explicitly enable background reconciliation. Replies and follow-ups must belong to a verified conversation and require a new approval.

When something fails

An uncertain send or meeting creation must be reconciled before another attempt. A lost response does not prove that nothing happened. Sync the mailbox or retry the same meeting details to look for the original provider record. Recorded AI results recover on return; unknown costs remain reserved.

If the account expires, sign in again. Reconnect a changed Google account in settings. Cloud billing has its own pending-operation recovery controls; use those instead of starting another purchase.

Membership and billing

Owners manage billing and ownership. Viewers can read and export shared CRM information; they are not restricted external recipients. Every workspace has its own Outreachr subscription. Your personal Eliza plan does not unlock Outreachr. Removing a member does not automatically reduce purchased seats.

Export, archive and leave

CSV exports and encrypted cloud archives remain available after the trial. Cloud archives include CRM records and saved documents, excluding temporary uploads, credentials, memberships and billing authority. Keep your password: the application cannot restore an archive without it. Restore requires a workspace without prior email or calendar history; reconnect accounts and review drafts afterward.

Archiving pauses editing, AI and sending, but does not cancel billing. Leaving removes your access and mailbox selection. Owners can permanently remove workspace content after paid billing ends. Minimal financial, membership and audit records remain, and the shared Eliza identity is unchanged.

Report a problem

Settings provides a redacted diagnostics download with revision, operation counts and billing state. Review it before sharing. Include the request ID displayed with an error. Public bug reports must contain only nonsensitive reproduction steps; never post credentials, prospect details, message bodies, documents or payment information.
